About

Background

Tori Ugalde is a choreographer, teacher, and costume designer based in Orlando, Florida. She trained across three competitive seasons at Celebration Arts Academy under director and choreographer Dixon Van Hoozer-Bowles, performing 20 pieces across contemporary, lyrical, jazz, musical theatre, and hip hop.

Her competition record includes 1st Overall at StarQuest, 1st Place at ASH Nationals, and Diamond awards at Groove. Now she channels that performance experience into choreography, teaching, and costume design — building the next generation of dancers while continuing to create.
