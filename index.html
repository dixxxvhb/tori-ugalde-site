About Tori

Tori Ugalde is a competitive dancer, emerging choreographer, and teacher based in Central Florida. Trained across three seasons at Celebration Arts Academy under director and choreographer Dixon Van Hoozer-Bowles, Tori built a versatile performance vocabulary spanning contemporary, lyrical, jazz, musical theatre, and hip hop. Her command of artistry and stage presence earned her some of the most recognized titles in the regional circuit, including 1st Overall at StarQuest and 1st Place at ASH Nationals — across 20 competition pieces in three seasons.

Beyond competing, Tori has stepped fully into the creative and educational side of the art form. She teaches and choreographs, bringing the same precision and intentionality that shaped her own training into the work she makes with other dancers. She also designs and creates dance costumes, merging technical craft with visual storytelling. Tori brings that full range — performer, maker, teacher — into every room she enters.
