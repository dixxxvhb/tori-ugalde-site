Tori Ugalde

Dancer · Choreographer · Teacher

Performer, maker, teacher — she brings that full range into every room she enters.

Tori Ugalde is a dancer, choreographer, and teacher based in Orlando, Florida. Trained across three seasons at Celebration Arts Academy under the direction of Dixon Van Hoozer-Bowles, she built a versatile performance vocabulary spanning contemporary, lyrical, jazz, musical theatre, and hip hop — earning 1st Overall at StarQuest and 1st Place at ASH Nationals across 20 competition pieces.

Beyond competing, Tori has stepped fully into the creative and educational side of the art form. She choreographs and teaches with the same precision and intentionality that shaped her own training. She also designs and creates dance costumes, merging technical craft with visual storytelling.
