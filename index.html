About

Background

Choreographer, teacher, and costume designer based in Orlando, Florida. Trained across three competitive seasons at Celebration Arts Academy under director Dixon Van Hoozer-Bowles.

20 competition pieces across contemporary, lyrical, jazz, musical theatre, and hip hop. 1st Overall at StarQuest. 1st Place at ASH Nationals. Diamond awards at Groove.
