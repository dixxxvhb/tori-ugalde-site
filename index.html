About

I grew up in competition dance. Now I build the dancers who win.

Three seasons at Celebration Arts Academy under director Dixon Van Hoozer-Bowles. 20 competition pieces across contemporary, lyrical, jazz, musical theatre, and hip hop. 1st Overall at StarQuest. 1st Place at ASH Nationals.

That background is what I bring to your studio, your dancers, and your stage. I choreograph pieces that compete at the highest level, I teach technique that builds real artists, and I design costumes that complete the vision.
